Turn Timer makes time visible with a colorful countdown and reusable sequences for game turns, routines and everyday tasks.

START SIMPLY
Use the quick visual timer or choose an editable starter: Game Night, Recipe Steps, Plant Watering, Classroom Stations, Meeting Agenda or Morning Routine.

BUILD YOUR SEQUENCE
Give rounds a name, duration, color, sound and emoji. Choose which rounds count as turns, repeat a sequence, pause, skip, restart or do over a turn.

KEEP THE SETUPS YOU REUSE
Save custom templates, return to recent session history and import portable .turntimer files without overwriting existing templates.

TURN TIMER PRO
The quick timer, starter templates, basic playback, five recent history records and one custom saved template are free. A one-time Pro purchase unlocks unlimited saved templates, full history and export, sharing, iCloud template and history sync, and template widgets.

No subscription, ads or third-party tracking SDKs. Core use does not require an app account. Pro sync uses your private iCloud database through your Apple account.
