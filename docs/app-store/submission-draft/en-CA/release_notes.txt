New in Turn Timer 1.1:

- Measure routine steps with a stopwatch on iPhone and iPad. Save recordings, compare times with targets and apply measured durations to a template.
- Review sessions as a timeline with pauses, or see totals by name in Share, Average and Turns views.
- Join and control a live routine from your paired iPhone or Apple Watch while connected. Update both apps to use this feature.
- Drag the idle iPhone timer rim to set its duration and opt in to local completion alerts.
- Return to started quick timers, playing sessions and unsaved template edits after closing the iPhone app.
- Updated timer controls, sequence progress and editor round colours.

Stopwatch recordings stay on the device where you save them. An unfinished stopwatch recording is not restored after restarting the app.
