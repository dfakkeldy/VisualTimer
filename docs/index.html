In your browser

Ready when you are.

Use a quick countdown or run a whole sequence. Save templates, review local history, and move templates between devices with a .turntimer file.

Install the web app on your Home Screen or desktop. Your templates and history stay in this browser unless you export them.

With Pro, the native app adds Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and iCloud sync. An Apple Watch companion is in testing. The quick timer, starter templates, and one custom saved template are free. Pro is a one-time purchase.
